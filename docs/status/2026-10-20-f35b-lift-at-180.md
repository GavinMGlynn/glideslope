### The F-35B's lift the same either side of 180 degrees, 2026-10-20

**What is still not true first.** Her lift past 90 degrees either way is
unsourced: nothing public gives the F-35B's lift in reversed air, and the
shape is this model's. It is lopsided, accepted as it is by the owner
(2026-10-21) because it leaves her flight inside 90 degrees either way and
her full-forward dive untouched: the symmetric shape moved the
wreck-prediction test from 4.2 to 9.7 cm, against its 5 cm bound (below). From 90 to 180 degrees she makes no
lift, as before. From -90 to -180 her lift falls straight from the -0.60 rad
value to nothing. A real wing met from its trailing edge makes some lift, and
makes it the same way on both sides. Her drag, and her lift from the
stabilator and flaperons (CLde, CLdroop), are not keyed to the angle of attack
this way and are not this change's.

**The cause.** Her lift table (`lift_table` in tools/make_f35b.py) ran from
-0.60 to 1.57 rad, and JSBSim holds a table's end value past its ends. So past
90 degrees her lift was nothing on one side, and from -0.60 down it was the
whole -0.30 rad of negative lift on the other. Flown backward, atan2(w, u)
crosses from 180 to -180 with each small pitch swing, and at 30 kt her lift
stepped by 1,229 lb (measured, below), which her height law took up.

**The fix, in tools/make_f35b.py**, run to make assets/jsbsim/aircraft/f35b/f35b.xml.
The table now runs all the way round. From -90 to 90 degrees it is what JSBSim
read before: the -0.60 value is held to -90, as it was held past the table's
end. Past -90 it runs straight to nothing at -180, and from 90 to 180 it is
nothing, as before, so it is continuous through 180. The argument is the same
as #221's for her pitching moment: the air from behind is the same whichever
side of 180 its angle is written.

**A first version, not kept.** That version was symmetric, falling straight
from -0.60 to nothing at -90 as the positive side falls at 90. It moved her
full-forward dive at 300 KCAS, which passes -0.60 rad, and CI's Rocky 9 shard
caught
`a_prediction_told_its_aircraft_is_a_wreck_holds_her_where_the_server_does_and_never_goes_to_a_nan`:
after the wreck the prediction was 9.7 cm from the server against its 5 cm
bound, where it had been 4.2 cm. The second version leaves flight within 90
degrees untouched. With it the test is back to 4.2 cm, the wreck at the same
step (495) as before.

**Verification** (linux-release):
`the_f35bs_lift_flown_backward_is_the_same_either_side_of_180_degrees` holds
her level at 300 ft in air moving past her from behind at 30 kt. It sweeps the
air's slope a degree at a time, so that her angle of attack is each whole
degree from 170 to 179 and from -179 to -170: twenty, ten each side,
asserted. The stated bound is that her lift moves under 50 lb from each
degree to the next, through 180 included: nineteen steps, asserted.
Measured: at most 14.8 lb a degree, 9.5 lb from 179 to -179, and her lift at
most 136 lb at -170. Seen red with the old model restored: 1,229 lb from 179
to -179.

"Every figure in range": with the new table, her published-figure tests, the
STOVL, hover, transition and prediction tests (`-R "f35b|stovl|predict"`),
the check that the model is what its script makes, and the selftest all pass.
The selftest's hash does not move: it flies the C172P.
