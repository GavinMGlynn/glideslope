### The F-35B's lift the same either side of 180 degrees, 2026-10-20

**What is still not true first.** Her lift past -0.60 rad and past 90
degrees either way is this model's shape, not a published one: nothing
public gives the F-35B's lift in reversed air. In this table she makes no
lift at all with the air from behind, where a real wing met from the
trailing edge makes some. Her drag and her lift from the stabilator and
flaperons (CLde, CLdroop) are not keyed to the angle of attack this way and
are not this change's.

**The cause.** Her lift table (`lift_table` in tools/make_f35b.py) ran from
-0.60 to 1.57 rad, and JSBSim holds a table's end value past its ends. So
past 90 degrees her lift was nothing on one side, and from -0.60 down it was
the whole -0.30 rad of negative lift on the other. Flown backward, atan2(w, u)
crosses from 180 to -180 with each small pitch swing, and at 30 kt her lift
stepped by 1,229 lb (measured, below), which her height law took up.

**The fix, in tools/make_f35b.py**, run to make assets/jsbsim/aircraft/f35b/f35b.xml.
The table now runs all the way round. From -0.60 up it is as it was. From
-0.60 it runs straight to nothing at -90 degrees, as the positive side already
fell to nothing at 90, and it is nothing from 90 to 180 either way. The same
argument as #221's for her pitching moment: the air from behind is the same
whichever side of 180 its angle is written.

**Verification** (linux-release):
`the_f35bs_lift_flown_backward_is_the_same_either_side_of_180_degrees` holds
her level at 300 ft in air moving past her from behind at 30 kt. It sweeps the
air's slope a degree at a time, so that her angle of attack is each whole
degree from 170 to 179 and from -179 to -170: twenty, ten each side,
asserted. The stated bound is her lift within 50 lb of nothing at each, and
so the step from 179 to -179 under 100 lb. Measured: 0.0 lb at all twenty.
Seen red with the old model restored: -1,229 lb at -179.3 degrees, at most
1,265 lb, against 0 on the 179 side.

"Every figure in range": her published-figure tests and the STOVL, hover and
transition tests (`-R "f35b|stovl"`) pass with the new table, as do the
model-made-by-its-script check and the selftest. The selftest's hash does not
move: it flies the C172P.
